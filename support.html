Quick answers

Common support questions.

What should I include in an order support message?

Include your order number if you have one, the email used at checkout, and a short description of what needs to be checked.

Can I call or text Knight Labs?

Phone support is marked as coming soon until a dedicated public support number is available. Email support is the current primary method.

Should I send payment or account credentials?

No. Do not send bank logins, passwords, private account credentials, or sensitive payment information through support messages.
