About Knight Labs

Who we are and how we work.

We supply research-grade peptides to laboratories, universities and independent researchers, with clear pricing, lot-level records and a real person to talk to.

Our role

A short supply chain, not a marketplace.

We stock a deliberately narrow catalog rather than listing everything available. That means we know what is on the shelf, what condition it has been kept in, and where it came from — and it is why we can answer a question about a specific vial rather than pointing you at a supplier.

Handling and storage

Peptides are sensitive to heat and light. Stock is kept in controlled cold storage, handled in short trips, and packed so it stays that way in transit.

Traceable to the lot

Each product is tracked by lot, so if you need to know the origin of what you received, we can tell you rather than guess.

How we work

No surprises, at any point in the order.

You should know the price, the size and the total before you commit, and be able to reach someone afterwards.
